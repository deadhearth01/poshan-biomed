# Poshan — Nutrition Question Artwork Prompt File

Companion to `MASTER_PROMPT.md`, which governs the app's overall illustration style. This
file covers the nutrition questionnaire: one illustration per question, in the same visual
language as the milestone artwork so a caregiver moving between modules never feels they
have changed app.

**Two sets, and they are different jobs:**

- **Feeding practice** (36 images) — one per question, per age band. Shown large, beside the
  question. These carry the meaning: many caregivers using this app read slowly, and the
  picture is often what the question actually communicates.
- **Background risk factors** (19 images) — smaller, quieter, section-level. These are
  sensitive topics and the artwork must not caricature poverty.

---

## 0. House style

Inherited from `MASTER_PROMPT.md` and repeated here so this file stands alone:

```
Flat vector children's-book illustration, soft rounded shapes, warm and friendly,
generous negative space, no outlines heavier than the shapes themselves,
muted natural palette with one warm accent, no text of any kind,
no logos, no brand marks, square composition, sRGB.
```

Non-negotiables for this module specifically:

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Indian families, homes, clothing, food** | This is the whole user base. Generic Western kitchens read as someone else's app |
| **Real Indian foods** — dal, rice, roti, idli, ragi, banana, egg, leafy greens | A caregiver should recognise their own food on screen |
| **Never depict a distressed or visibly malnourished child** | The caregiver answering is the child's parent. Showing a suffering child to ask about feeding is cruel and makes people stop answering honestly |
| **No text, no numbers, no measuring scales with readings** | Untranslatable, and the app already carries the numbers |
| **Steel and brass utensils, not ceramic bowls** | The right vessel is part of the recognition |
| **Mother and baby both look calm and well** | These questions are about practice, not accusation |

Deliver **1024×1024 PNG**, named exactly as the drawable id in the tables below.

## 2. Background risk factors — 19 images

**These need the most careful handling in the whole app.** They ask about poverty, maternal
education, sanitation and cooking fuel. The illustration must depict a *circumstance*,
never a judgement, and never a person who looks pitiable.

Rules specific to this set:

- **Depict the object or situation, not a suffering person.** A water source, a cooking
  stove, a toilet — not a distressed family
- **Homes are modest but cared-for.** Swept floors, neat utensils, a plant. Poverty is not
  the same as squalor, and drawing it as squalor tells the family using the app what you
  think of them
- **No sad faces anywhere in this set.** Neutral or gently positive only
- **Softer, smaller, quieter** than the feeding-practice set — these sit at section level

## 4. Note on what artwork cannot do

An illustration can show *what is being asked*. It cannot make a question safe to ask
badly. The background set in particular is asked **once per child**, not at every visit,
and the app must say why it is asking — these factors are associated with undernutrition
risk at population level, not a prediction about the child in front of you. The artwork
should support that framing, not undercut it with imagery that implies blame.
